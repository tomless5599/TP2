ℹ️ How to Use

What is a bot dump? Bot dumps occur when automated bots sell large quantities of items quickly, causing sudden price drops. This tool helps you spot these opportunities.

  • Search for items: Type an item name and click Search to find items
  • Add to watchlist: Click on an item from search results to add it to your watchlist
  • Monitor prices: The tracker will automatically update prices every 30 seconds
  • Bot dump alerts: Items with sudden price drops (>5%) and high volume will be flagged with a red alert
  • Buy low, sell high: Bot dumps often recover within hours, making them good flip opportunities

Bot Dump Indicators:

  • Price drop of 5% or more in 5 minutes
  • Trading volume 2x higher than normal
  • Items with these indicators will pulse red
